Popular Help Topics

Study Assistance
How do I access course materials and textbooks?
−
To access study materials, navigate to the Textbooks or Quick Revision sections from the top navigation bar. Select your educational board (CBSE, ICSE, or UP Board) and choose your target subject to immediately view or download high-resolution notes and chapters.
What do I do if I need last-minute revision formula sheets?
+
Our 'Quick Revision' section offers condensed formula flashcards and summary study sheets specifically curated for fast examination prep across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and more.
Can I download PDFs for offline study reading?
+
Yes! When viewing any textbook or coaching note in our PDF viewer, simply click the Download button in the toolbar to save a permanent local copy directly to your desktop or mobile device.
Account & Profile
How do I set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
+
Go to your account profile settings and select 'Security & 2FA'. You can enroll your preferred authenticator app or email OTP verification to protect your saved bookmarks and student progress.
How do I reset my forgotten student password?
+
Click 'Log In' from the navigation bar, then select 'Forgot Password?'. Enter your registered email address to receive a secure recovery verification link immediately.
Academic Shelf & Bookmarks
Where are my bookmarked chapters saved?
+
Whenever you click the bookmark ribbon icon on any textbook or document, it is automatically saved to your 'My Shelf' bookmarks page for one-click jumping back into your study session.
Technical Support
What if a PDF document fails to load or shows a blank page?
+
Please verify your internet connection and clear your browser cache. If an asset continues to malfunction, report the specific textbook title using the 'Send Us a Message' ticket form on the right.
