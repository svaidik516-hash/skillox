How to Setup 2FA

Follow these three simple steps to link your mobile smartphone with Skillox High-Security authentication:

1
Download an Authenticator App Install Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy from your iPhone App Store or Google Play Store.
2
Scan QR Code or Type Secret Open the app, tap the "+" (Add) button, choose "Scan a QR code", and point your phone camera at the QR code shown on this screen. (Or choose "Enter setup key" and paste the alphanumeric code displayed under the image).
3
Enter the 6-Digit Code Your app will instantly show a rotating 6-digit verification code for Skillox. Enter that code in the boxes below and click "Activate Authenticator Protection".
